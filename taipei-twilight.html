Taipei Twilight

Close your eyes, and you're in Taipei.

Bergamot · Sweet Orange · Rosemary · Lavender

Warm humidity in the night air.
Scooters pass by the alley.
A cup of black tea still warm in your hands.

Some cities never really sleep — they soften.
Taipei glows quietly after midnight,
not loud, not silent, just alive enough
to keep you company.
